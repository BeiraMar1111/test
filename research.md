# Research — Full-Arch Dental Implant Landing Pages

Quick competitive scan of the niche (full-arch / All-on-4 dental implant clinics), focused on conversion-oriented design patterns rather than any single site to copy.

## What strong pages in this niche do

- **Real, warm portrait photography over clinical stock shots.** Close-up stock teeth photography reads cold and slightly off-putting for a procedure people are often self-conscious about. Pages that convert lean on real patients smiling, in natural light, not lab-coat/equipment imagery up front.
- **Social proof placed high, not buried.** Trust signals (review ratings, years in practice, number of procedures done, certifications) sit near the top of the page, before the visitor has to scroll to find a reason to trust the clinic.
- **Serif headline / sans-serif body pairing** shows up repeatedly as a way to carry emotional weight in the headline while keeping supporting copy easy to scan.
- **Warm, non-clinical color palettes.** The cold blue-and-white "medical" palette is common but not what the better-converting pages use — warmer, more human palettes perform better for an elective, confidence-driven procedure.
- **Dedicated procedure-specific pages**, not a generic homepage. Clinics that do this well send paid/search traffic to a page built around one procedure (e.g. All-on-4) rather than a general practice homepage, so the message stays singular.
- **The headline sells the outcome, not the procedure.** Benefit- and identity-led headlines ("get your life back," "one visit, new smile") outperform procedure-first headlines ("All-on-4 Dental Implants") for top-of-funnel conversion.
- **Booking/consultation CTA is visible immediately**, not just at the bottom — usually in the hero and repeated at every major scroll stop.

## What to avoid

- Leading with a generic stock photo of teeth or a smile with no face/context.
- Burying pricing/financing information — cost is a top anxiety for this procedure and pages that dodge it too long lose trust.
- A wall of clinical/technical copy before any emotional hook or social proof.
- Cold, sterile "hospital" visual language for what is ultimately a confidence and lifestyle purchase.
