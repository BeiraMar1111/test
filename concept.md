# Concept — Page Structure & Copy Direction

## 1. Page structure

1. **Hero (above the fold)** — headline about confidence/a new smile in one visit, subheadline naming the key differentiator (e.g. "All-on-4 in a single day"), primary CTA ("Get Your Free Consultation"), background photo of a real patient/smile, not stock teeth clip art.
2. **Trust bar** — certification logos, "X years in practice," "X successful implants placed," review rating (Google/Trustpilot).
3. **Problem → solution** — short: "Living with problem teeth is hard" → "A full-arch solution in one visit."
4. **How treatment works** — 3–4 steps, visual (consultation → 3D scan → surgery → result).
5. **Before/after** — real (or representative) cases, visually strong block.
6. **Doctor/clinic** — doctor photo, credentials, a personal note (builds trust for an expensive procedure).
7. **Patient testimonials** — video or text, with names.
8. **FAQ** — addresses common fears (pain, cost, financing, timeline).
9. **Financing/CTA block** — "0% financing available" if applicable, CTA repeated.
10. **Final CTA + contact/form**

## 2. Copywriting direction

- Tone: confident, warm, not clinical — speaking to someone self-conscious about their smile, not to "a patient."
- Hero direction (not final copy): *"Your New Smile. One Visit. Zero Compromise."*
- Emphasize **speed** (one visit instead of months) and **confidence** (life change), not medical detail.
- CTA is consistent in meaning everywhere but not word-for-word repeated: "Book Your Free Consultation" / "See If You Qualify" / "Start Your Transformation."
