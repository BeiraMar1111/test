## 1. Research Summary

### Clinics & references reviewed (Sep 2025)

| Clinic | Region | Notable patterns |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| ClearChoice | US | Lifetime warranty, zirconia focus, tiered arch products, emotional "confidence for life" messaging |
| Nuvia | US | Speed-led ("24 hours"), eligibility quiz, heavy video/social proof, conversion-first layout |
| Carolina Implants | US | Transparent tiered pricing on-page, proprietary workflow branding (Innova), digital planning |
| Smileloc | US | All-inclusive pricing, anti-surprise positioning, specialist team emphasis |
| Fusion Dental Implants | US | Price anchoring vs competitors, lifetime warranty, monthly payment framing |
| Spring Hope Dentistry | US | Radical pricing transparency, consultation fee credited, geographic reach copy |
| Perth Dental Implant Centre | AU | Package tiers (Basic/Essential/Advanced), Centre of Excellence credential |
| DentAkademi / Pro Dental / Norton | UK (Harley St) | Local trust, 0% finance, hospitality touches (hotel, transport), specialist credentials |
| Sofia Implant Centre | EU | Volume/specialisation proof, low price anchoring, warranty tiers |
| Smilo Dental | UK | Multi-location, warranty, sedation, long experience lists |

### A. Common patterns

- **Hero**: Outcome-led headline + consultation CTA; often price or speed visible above fold
- **Trust**: Credentials, years of experience, implant counts, review scores, regulatory badges
- **Treatment**: All-on-4 explained with implant count + fixed bridge benefit
- **Process**: 4–5 step timeline from consult → scan → surgery → temp → final
- **Proof**: Before/after galleries, video testimonials, Google reviews
- **Pricing**: Ranges, packages, "all-inclusive" language, financing/monthly payments
- **CTA repetition**: Form + phone at hero, mid-page, and footer
- **FAQ**: Candidacy, pain, cost, timeline, dentures

### B. Common UX problems

- **Visual sameness**: Blue/white palettes, stock smiles, interchangeable layouts
- **Information overload**: 15–18 sections, icon grids, competing CTAs
- **Premature price anchoring**: Cost before trust often increases bounce for anxious researchers
- **Credential walls**: Long bullet lists without human context
- **Aggressive urgency**: Countdown timers, "limited offer" — erodes premium feel
- **Generic empathy copy**: "We care about your smile" without specificity
- **Mobile friction**: Forms too long; before/after too small; sticky CTAs missing

### C. Conversion opportunities

- **Consultation specificity**: "Private consultation" + what's included reduces form anxiety
- **Written plan promise**: Differentiator vs clinics that vague-quote until chairside
- **Interactive journey**: Tabbed timeline reduces cognitive load vs vertical wall of steps
- **Before/after interaction**: Drag slider increases engagement and time-on-page
- **Objection-ordered FAQ**: Match question sequence to research journey (candidacy → pain → look → cost)
- **Dual conversion paths**: Form for planners, click-to-call for high-intent (per Brighton Implant CRO analysis)
- **Post-proof CTA placement**: Primary CTA after journey + results sections (per TYPZA implant UX research)

### D. Visual opportunities

- **Editorial typography**: Serif display + clean sans body — hospitality/luxury healthcare crossover
- **Warm neutral palette**: Stone, cream, sage, bronze — avoids clinical blue cliché
- **Asymmetric hero grid**: Photography composition vs centred template
- **Restrained photography**: Lifestyle + subtle clinical; not mouth macro shots
- **Negative space**: Premium feel through spacing, not decoration
- **Single accent system**: Deep sage CTA + bronze overlines — cohesive, not rainbow UI

### E. Deliberately avoided

- Blue/white dental template aesthetic
- Fake statistics, awards, patient counts, star ratings
- "Your smile journey" / generic wellness language
- Icon-card grids for every benefit
- Pop-ups, chat widgets, urgency banners
- Price in hero (deferred to FAQ — trust first)
- Eligibility quiz gating (feels salesy for premium audience)

### F. Differentiation for Arcwell

1. **Specialist-only positioning** — full-arch exclusively, not general dentistry
2. **Calm editorial tone** — luxury hospitality meets clinical precision
3. **Transparency without price-led hero** — written plan promise before numbers
4. **Human-first photography direction** — whole person, not disembodied teeth
5. **Interactive education** — journey tabs + before/after slider vs static cards
6. **Named clinical continuity** — same team from consult to completion

## 2. Key UX Insights

- Full-arch patients research for weeks; they compare 3–5 clinics before contacting any
- Primary anxiety clusters: **Am I suitable? Will it hurt? Will it look fake? What will it cost? Can I trust them?**
- Decision triggers: clear process, credible specialist, relatable results, no-pressure consultation
- Best CTA framing: specific next step + low commitment ("private consultation, no obligation")
- Mobile research happens evenings; tap-to-call + short form essential

---

## 3. Target Audience / User Mindset

**Who**: Adults 45–75 (not exclusive), missing/failing teeth or long-term denture wearers

**Mindset on arrival**:
- "I've put this off for years"
- "I'm comparing options — everyone claims to be the best"
- "I'm scared of the cost and the pain"
- "I need to understand this before I talk to anyone"

**Success state**: "These people specialise in exactly my problem. I understand the process. I trust them. I know what booking means."

## 4. Homepage Strategy

| Element | Decision |
|---------|----------|
| Primary conversion | Book private consultation (form) |
| Secondary conversion | Phone call |
| Core value prop | Full-arch specialists who give you a clear, written path — fixed teeth designed for your life |
| Trust strategy | Credentials (placeholder), specialist focus, process transparency, patient voices, clinical approach |
| Conversion strategy | Educate → reassure → prove → handle objections → ask |
| Tone | Confident, calm, direct — never salesy |

### Hero directions explored

| Direction | Headline | Verdict |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| Speed-led | "Permanent teeth. One day to begin." | Rejected — competes with Nuvia; feels transactional |
| Technical | "All-on-4.full-arch restoration specialists" | Rejected — jargon-first |
| Emotional | "Stop living around your teeth." | Strong — used in problem section |
| **Selected** | **"Fixed teeth that feel like yours again."** | Benefit + permanence + personal; premium tone |

## 5. Information Architecture

```
Header (sticky)
├── Hero — what + primary CTA
├── Trust strip — immediate credibility
├── Problem — relevance ("is this me?")
├── Treatment — what All-on-4/full-arch means
├── Journey — how it works (interactive)
├── Results — before/after proof
├── Approach — clinical differentiation
├── Clinician — human trust anchor
├── Testimonials — social proof
├── FAQ — objection handling
├── CTA + form — conversion
└── Footer
```

**Sections omitted** (deliberately):
- Pricing table in hero — deferred to FAQ
- Financing calculator — placeholder only
- Blog/content — not homepage priority
- Multiple doctor grid — one lead profile for focus

## 7. Visual Design Direction

### Typography
- **Display**: Cormorant Garamond — editorial, refined
- **Body**: Instrument Sans — modern, readable
- **Hierarchy**: Display XL (hero) → Display MD (sections) → Body LG (leads) → Body SM (support)

### Color
| Token | Value | Use |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Background | `#F6F2EB` | Warm cream base |
| Text | `#1A1814` | Primary copy |
| Accent | `#3D4F44` | CTA, journey panel |
| Bronze | `#A68452` | Overlines, accents |

### Components
- **Buttons**: 4px radius, no pill shapes — architectural, not SaaS
- **Cards**: Subtle border, minimal shadow on hover
- **Images**: 16px radius, editorial crops
- **Spacing**: 8px base; section gaps 80–128px desktop

## 8. Mobile Direction

| Element | Desktop | Mobile |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Navigation | Horizontal links + CTA | Hamburger → full-screen overlay |
| Hero | 2-column grid | Stacked; content first, image second |
| CTAs | Inline buttons | Full-width; sticky bar (Call + Book) |
| Journey | Side tabs + panel | Stacked tabs; panel below |
| Before/after | Large slider | Full-width; touch drag |
| Form | Right column | Full-width below copy |
| Typography | Display clamp scales down | Hero ~2.75rem min |

## 10. Interaction / Motion

- **Scroll reveal**: Sections fade up on enter (respects `prefers-reduced-motion` — add in production)
- **Before/after slider**: Pointer drag comparison
- **Journey tabs**: Click to swap panel content
- **FAQ accordion**: Expand/collapse with aria states
- **Header**: Background blur on scroll
- **Buttons**: Subtle lift on hover
- **Sticky mobile CTA**: Appears ≤768px

---

## 11. Key Design Decisions

1. **Warm sage over clinical blue** — instant visual differentiation
2. **No hero pricing** — trust before numbers for high-consideration audience
3. **Single clinician focus** — depth over breadth
4. **Placeholder honesty** — no fabricated credentials or stats
5. **Editorial hero layout** — asymmetric grid vs template centre-align
6. **Separate entry point** — `dental.html` preserves existing project

## 12. Post-Launch Testing Recommendations

| Test | Hypothesis |
|------|------------|
| Hero headline A/B | "Fixed teeth that feel like yours again" vs "Full-arch implants. One clear path forward." |
| CTA copy | "Book a private consultation" vs "Request your treatment plan" |
| Consultation form length | 5 fields vs 3 fields (+ follow-up call) |
| Before/after position | Above vs below journey section |
| Trust strip | With vs without review placeholder |
| Phone prominence | Sticky call button vs form-only mobile |
| FAQ order | Candidacy-first vs cost-first |
| Scroll depth | Where drop-off occurs — optimize content order |

**Metrics to track**: Consultation form submissions, click-to-call, scroll depth, time on journey section, before/after interaction rate, bounce rate by traffic source.
